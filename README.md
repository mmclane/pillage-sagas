# Saga Season — A Campaign for Pillage

A persistent multiplayer campaign layer for *Pillage: Ransack the Middle Ages* (Triskell Interactive, Guillaume Rousselot). Designed for clubs of 3 to 6 players. Every battle is player versus player; no NPC defenders. Warbands grow and decay across a season, named characters earn XP and Talents, and the player whose saga ends with the most renown is sung about in the long winter.

**Version:** 0.5 (draft, design in progress)

## Where we are

- **v0.1 covered** the engine: warband structure, post-game sequence, XP/Talents conversion, Boasts deck mechanic, Stores list, Relics list with transfer rules.
- **v0.2 added** the three random tables (Saga Injury d20, Saga Roll d100, Title d10 × d6) and recorded the resolved design decisions.
- **v0.3 expanded** the Boast deck to 67 cards, wrote the 12 Battle Types with full detail, drafted the Saga Advancement Table, designed the Annual Events deck (30 cards), and added 18 Locations.
- **v0.5 polished** the docs with an Edge Cases & Rulings file, added Forgo Advancement, Legendary Titles, Lasting Injury → Title auto-mappings, optional Title effects, 3 Multi-player Battle Types, 3 Faction-flavored Battle Types, 6 Christendom Stores, 7 more Relics, 5 more Annual Events, and 6 more Boasts. The framework is essentially feature-complete for first playtest.
- **Open design questions** are tracked in [07_Design_Notes.md](07_Design_Notes.md) so we don't lose threads.
