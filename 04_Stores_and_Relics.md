# 04 — Stores and Relics

Two layers of campaign-only gear:

- **Stores** are single-use consumables. Bought with gp, expended in battle, gone forever.
- **Relics** are persistent, unique, named artifacts with strong bonuses. Few in number, transferable between warbands via capture, theft, or ransom.

---

## Part A — Stores (Single-Use Consumables)

### A1. Rules

- Stores carry between battles until used.
- Cap at **3 Stores fielded per battle** (you may own more, but only bring 3 to any one game).
- All Stores must be declared and assigned to specific figures during the battle's army-build step, before the first turn.
- Stores are bought in the post-game **Recruit and Re-equip** step.

## Part B — Named Relics

Persistent, unique, named items with significant bonuses. Fewer than Stores, more powerful, transferable between warbands.

### B1. How Relics enter play

- **Pre-seeded:** 3 to 5 Relics are placed in player rosters at campaign start by random draw, scenario placement, or as defender's possession in certain scenarios.
- **From the Saga Roll:** Specific d100 entries generate Relics ("an ancient sword is found in a burial mound").
- **From scenarios:** Some scenarios (like Winding Ways' Sack of Saint Lunaire) end with the victor controlling the church's relic, which becomes a campaign Relic.
- **From Legendary Boasts:** Some Legendary Boasts, on completion, create a Relic (e.g. *Slayer of Jarls* generates a unique sword named after the slain Jarl).

Cap: about **8 to 10 active Relics** at any time across the campaign. Excess generation rolls a discard.

### B2. How Relics work in play

- A Relic must be **assigned** to a specific Named Character. They wield it.
- The bonus applies as long as the wielder is on the table and not Down.
- A Relic may have an equipment-slot type (sword, banner, amulet, helmet, etc.), but the Relic does not cost army-building gp. It is a free upgrade on top of the wielder's existing equipment.
- **One Relic per wielder.** A character may not hold multiple Relics.
- Players may freely **reassign Relics within their own warband** between battles.

### B3. Transfer between warbands

**If the wielder is killed outright in battle (no capture):**
The Relic drops at the spot of death. Functions exactly like a loot token: any infantry figure can pick it up by reaching it. If carried off the table by an enemy, that warband owns the Relic. If still on the table at game end, the Relic returns to its original owner's warband.

**If the wielder is captured alive (taken prisoner):**
The captor immediately controls the Relic. In the post-game Ransom step, the captor chooses:

- **Demand the Relic plus standard ransom.** Prisoner is returned, but the Relic stays with the captor. Original owner pays half-cost ransom AND loses the Relic.
- **Demand only ransom (Relic for sale separately).** Prisoner is returned with the Relic, but the captor demands an additional fee (negotiated, default 100 gp) to release the Relic.
- **Refuse all ransom.** Execute or sacrifice the prisoner and keep the Relic permanently. The killer gains a **Bitter Enmity** mark from the victim's warband.
