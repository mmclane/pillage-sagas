# 03 — Boasts (Private Objectives)

Each player draws a private **Boast card** before each battle. Boasts encourage rare, dramatic, or thematic actions and reward Renown when completed. They are the campaign's "do something cinematic" engine.

---

## 1. Drawing Boasts

- At the start of every battle, the player draws **2 Boasts and keeps 1** (the other is discarded face-down to the bottom of the deck).
- A player may **discard their Boast at deployment** for a one-time mulligan per game if the matchup makes it clearly impossible. Draw 1 replacement.
- Boasts are **private** until claimed. Kept face-down in front of the player.
- A **multi-game Boast** (marked with a moon icon) rolls over into the next battle if not completed. Worth more Renown but takes up a permanent hand slot.
- **Hand size cap: 2 active Boasts** (1 from standard draw + 1 from any other source).

---

## 2. Claiming a Boast

When the trigger condition is met during a game, the player reveals the card. The Renown is awarded immediately. The opponent sees what was claimed. Completed Boasts go to a personal **Saga Pile** for season-end recognition.

> **Open design question:** Public-reveal-on-trigger (current default) vs. fully secret claim at game end. Recommend: public-on-trigger. More dramatic, harder to cheese.

---

## 3. Refreshing Boasts between battles

- **Standard:** Draw 2 keep 1 before each battle (baseline).
- **Mead-Hall (gp spend):** Discard your held Boast and re-draw at cost: 20 gp for 1-keep-1, 50 gp for 2-keep-1, 100 gp for 3-keep-1.
- **Renown spend:** Spend 1 Renown to draw an extra Boast (kept up to the hand size cap).
- **Saga Roll grants:** Some d100 results grant free Boast draws.
- **Legendary completion:** Completing a Legendary Boast grants a free immediate redraw.
- **Mead-Hall Holding** (Holdings Mode only): owning one grants one free Boast swap per season.

---

## 4. Tiers

| Tier | Renown reward | Difficulty | Typical |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Common** | 1 | Achievable most games with intent | Use a Healer's heal successfully; Set a building on fire |
| **Bold** | 2 | Requires specific build choices or risk | Kill an FA figure with a slinger; End a turn on a building roof |
| **Legendary** | 3 | Narrative gold, often single-game-defining | Kill the enemy Jarl in single combat with no allies within 6"; Survive a Dane axe hit then kill the wielder |

Approximate deck split: 50% Common, 35% Bold, 15% Legendary.

---

## 5. The Deck (v0.3)

67 cards organized by tier. Each entry shows the trigger condition; Renown reward follows the tier (Common = 1, Bold = 2, Legendary = 3). A 🌙 icon marks a multi-game Boast (carries over until completed or discarded; occupies a permanent hand slot).

**Restrictions.** Some Boasts are **faction-locked** (only completable by a warband of the named faction) or **Battle-Type-locked** (only completable in a specific Battle Type). If a player draws a Boast they clearly cannot complete given their warband or the current matchup, they may use their once-per-game **mulligan at deployment** (per §1) to discard and re-draw.

## 6. Edge cases and rulings

- **"Killed by your figures"** means your figure made the killing roll (the wound that took the target to 0 HP), regardless of supports or warhounds nearby.
- **Multi-game Boasts 🌙** stay in the player's hand between battles until completed or actively discarded (which counts as the player's once-per-game mulligan for that battle's standard draw). A multi-game Boast does not block the standard draw; it just occupies one of the 2 hand slots.
- **Slayer of Jarls (L01)** generates a Relic only on the first completion in the campaign. If completed again later, award the Renown but no second Relic.
- **Saga of Survival (L06)** resets if your Jarl ever goes Down during the streak. The 3 consecutive battles must include at least one battle where the Jarl was deployed and actively in danger (a battle the Jarl sat out doesn't count toward the streak).
- **Threefold Saga (L03) and Hall of the Fallen (L05)** require declaring which Named Character is the target when the Boast is drawn. The named character is locked in for the duration of the Boast.
- **One Against Many (L02), Last Stand (B09), Wyrd-Marked (L07)** all require the same character (your Jarl) to be in specific situations. A single game can complete multiple of these at once if the conditions overlap. Award Renown for each.
- **Sworn Brothers (C24)** requires the two Named Characters to be alive at the moment of game end, not just that they survived a previous turn. If both die on the last turn, the Boast fails.
- **First Blood (C14)** triggers only when the very first enemy is killed. If your opponent's figure dies first (to your hand), the Boast completes. If your figure dies first, the Boast fails (cannot be re-attempted later in the game).
- **Defender's Right (C17)** does not trigger if the enemy charging your figure rolled a failed charge (didn't reach). The enemy must successfully contact your figure with their charge.
- **A Pyre for the Dead (C21)** requires 2 buildings simultaneously burning at any moment. Buildings that have burned out (per scenario rules, if used) and are extinguished do not count.
- **Hall of the Fallen (L05) versus Slayer of Jarls (L01)** can overlap. If a single Named Character kills both a Huscarl and the Jarl in the same game, both Boasts trigger.
