# 09 — Quick Start

An onboarding guide for clubs starting a Saga Season campaign. Get from "we want to try this" to running your first battle in roughly 30 minutes of setup.

If you're reading the full ruleset, this file is your "tomorrow night" starter. The other docs are the reference.

---

## 1. What you need

**Players:** 3 to 6. Four is the sweet spot.

**Designated campaign organiser:** one person tracks Renown across warbands, runs Annual Events, arbitrates rules disputes. Can also play a warband.

**Per warband (each player):**
- A painted (or unpainted-but-distinguishable) Pillage army from any faction
- A Warband Sheet (see §6 below)
- d6, d10, d20 dice; d100 made from 2d10 if needed

**Shared at the table:**
- Boast deck (67 cards — printable from [03_Boasts.md §5](03_Boasts.md))
- Annual Events deck (30 cards — from [08_Annual_Events.md](08_Annual_Events.md))
- Reference sheets for Saga Roll, Saga Injury Table, Title Table (from [06_Tables.md](06_Tables.md))
- Tokens: loot, gp counters, Renown counters
- Terrain for the Locations you plan to use

**Terrain footprint:** the 18 Locations cover wide ground. Most clubs will own enough for: Open Steppe, Marshlands or Forest Edge, Coastal Village, Hall Compound, and one of (Stone Keep / Trading Town). Exotic Locations (Henge, Frozen Lake, Longship Boarding, etc.) can be banned at campaign start per the mutual-veto rule — you only need to support what you play.

## 2. Day 0 setup (30-60 minutes, one-time)

### Step 1: Pick a campaign mode

| Mode | Sounds like | Recommended for |
|---|---|---|
| **Two Sides Mode** | "Northmen vs Christendom; pick a side" | First-time campaigns. Built-in narrative. |
| **Feuds Mode** | "Free-for-all; everyone for themselves" | Clubs that want individual stories, or odd player counts that don't split evenly. |

See [01_Core_Rules.md §2](01_Core_Rules.md) for full mode details. The campaign engine is the same; the wrapper is what changes.

### Step 2: Decide season length

- **Short season (recommended):** 6 raids + Grand Finale. 7-8 meetings total. Roughly 2-3 months of weekly play.
- **Standard season:** 8 raids + Grand Finale. 9-10 meetings total.

### Step 3: Each player creates a warband

1. **Pick a faction** from Pillage core (Vikings, Anglo-Saxons, Normans, Irish/Scots/Picts, Carolingian Franks, Bretons, Welsh, Generic) or Fall of Rome supplement.
2. **Pick a warband name** (e.g., "The Wolves of Hedeby," "Brokenmoor's Sworn").
3. **Starting Treasury: 300 gp**, plus the standard army budget for your first battle.
4. **Build your Named Roster:**
   - Name **1 Chieftain candidate** (your default Chieftain, a General Named Character).
   - Name **2 to 4 additional Generals** (your battle bench — these are the people who can become Chieftain in future battles).
   - Optionally name **1 Healer**, **1 Banner-bearer**, and/or **1 Warhorn-bearer** (class-locked specialists).
5. **Distribute up to 2 starting Talents** across your Generals at standard gp prices (paid from your starting Treasury). Concentrate on one character or spread for flexibility — your call.
6. **Write everything on a Warband Sheet** (see §6 below for the template).

### Step 4: Establish the ban list

- Each player names **one Location** they don't want to play in (no terrain, no interest, etc.).
- **Mutual veto:** if either player has banned a Location, it can't appear in their battles.
- Bans are final for the season.

### Step 5: Pick a Holdings yield cadence

- **Per post-game** (recommended for fast clubs): Holdings yield gp/Renown after every battle the holder fights in.
- **Per Winter** (longer arcs): Holdings yield only at the end of the season.

### Step 6: Seed Relics (optional)

- The campaign organiser places **3 to 5 Relics** from the [Relic catalog](04_Stores_and_Relics.md) into the campaign's pool. These are either:
  - **Pre-assigned** by random draw (each warband may roll for one starting Relic), OR
  - **Held in pool** until a Sack the Monastery battle gives one to the winner

## 3. First meeting walkthrough (2.5-3 hours)

### Pre-meeting (5 min)

- Organiser brings: Annual Events deck, Boast deck, the three reference tables (Saga Roll, Saga Injury, Title).
- Each player brings: Warband Sheet, miniatures, dice.

### Step 1: Annual Event check (5 min)

Annual Events fire at **every other meeting** (meetings 1, 3, 5, 7 of an 8-meeting season). At those meetings:
- Organiser draws an Annual Event card and reveals it
- Apply effects per [08_Annual_Events.md](08_Annual_Events.md)
- Players may mitigate (spend Renown or gp) where allowed

### Step 2: Propose and pair (10 min)

Players publicly propose battles:
1. Any player announces a challenge: "I propose [Battle Type] at [Location], I am the [role], to [target player]."
2. Target responds: accept clean / accept with Twist / counter-propose / refuse.
3. Once accepted, the pair heads to a table.
4. Other players pair up the same way. A 6-player club night typically produces 3 simultaneous battles.

If a player isn't proposed to and doesn't propose, the organiser steps in with a random or unmatched-pair pairing.

### Step 3: Roll weather (1 min per pair)

Each pair rolls d6 on Pillage's weather table — unless a Twist already imposed weather (some Twists do).

### Step 4: Play the battle (~90 min)

Resolve per Pillage's standard rules, with:
- The Battle Type's victory conditions and special rules ([05_Scenarios.md §3](05_Scenarios.md))
- The Location's terrain and mechanical effects ([05_Scenarios.md §4](05_Scenarios.md))
- The Twist (if any) ([05_Scenarios.md §5](05_Scenarios.md))
- The Weather effect

Each player tracks Boasts completed, kills, Renown earned in real time.

### Step 5: Post-game sequence (~30 min per pair)

Each pair runs the 12-step post-game sequence ([01_Core_Rules.md §6](01_Core_Rules.md)):

1. **Wound Rolls** — fallen Named Characters roll on the Saga Injury Table
2. **Tally Renown** — add up from kills, Boasts, scenario objectives
3. **Collect Treasure** — loot tokens to gp
4. **Ransom & Relic Transfer** — resolve captives and Relic moves
5. **Saga Roll** — d100 for between-game event
6. **Divide Shares** — pay your warriors; Oathbreaking check if you can't
7. **Mead-Hall** — optional spend gp/Renown to refresh Boasts
8. **Recruit & Re-equip** — buy replacements, equipment, Stores
9. **Advancement** — Named Characters spend XP
10. **Title Check** — major victories roll on the Title Table
11. **Holdings Updates** — yield Holdings, resolve contested ones
12. **Draw Next Boasts** — refresh your Boast hand

Update Warband Sheets. Organiser updates the campaign tracker.

## 4. Sample first season: "The Cold Coast"

A 6-raid season in Two Sides Mode (Northmen vs Christendom). Use proposal/composition each meeting, or use these suggested scenarios as starting points for your first season.

| Meeting | Annual Event? | Suggested battles (composed via proposal) |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Yes — draw one | Pillage Town in Coastal Village; Cattle Raid in Open Steppe |
| **2** | No | Rival Warband Feud in Forest Edge; Sack the Monastery in Monastery Grounds |
| **3** | Yes — draw one | Sack the Hall in Hall Compound; Coast Watch Tower in Highland Crags |
| **4** | No | Pitched Battle in Open Steppe; Saga Duel in Henge |
| **5** | Yes — draw one | Storm the Keep in Stone Keep; Rescue the Hostage in Hall Compound |
| **6** | No | Wagon Train Ambush in Marshlands; Defend the Longship in Tidal Estuary |
| **7** | **GRAND FINALE** | All warbands field full Treasury and Roster. Sides Mode: coalition battle. Feuds Mode: multi-player free-for-all. Highest individual or side Renown wins the season. |

The suggested scenarios are starting points. If players want to propose differently in a given meeting, that's the design intent — the composition mechanic is the campaign's identity.

## 5. Tips for the campaign organiser

- **Keep a master sheet** with each player's faction, side (if applicable), Treasury total, Renown total, Holdings list, and any season-spanning Injuries (Bitter Enmity marks, Lingering Wounds, etc.). Update after each meeting.
- **Set a pace** — meetings should run 2.5-3 hours. If post-game sequences are stretching past 30 minutes, simplify or skip steps.
- **Encourage flavor** — the system rewards in-fiction grievances. Player A's "I propose Sack the Hall, in retaliation for your kinsman's killing" is the heart of the campaign.
- **Track Annual Events visibly** — write the current and most recent Annual Events somewhere all players can see.
- **Veto Custom Battle Types thoughtfully** — players will sometimes propose Custom Battle Types that look fair but exploit the rewards. Check that the reward category and the difficulty match.
- **Read the saga aloud** — when AE30 (The Saga Journal) fires, take 5 minutes for each player to describe their warband's deeds. This is the campaign's narrative payoff.
- **At season end** — read each warband's named characters with their Titles and fallen comrades in their saga's order. This is the closure that makes the campaign feel like a campaign.

## 7. Common questions

**Q: What if my warband loses every battle? Am I out?**
A: No. Renown floors at 0. Even losing players earn Renown from completed Boasts and participation. The Saga Journal Annual Event explicitly rewards persistence.

**Q: What if I can't make a meeting?**
A: Skip it. Your warband isn't penalized; your Renown gain pauses for that round. Holdings still yield (depending on cadence). Annual Events still affect you between meetings.

**Q: Can I switch warband factions mid-campaign?**
A: No. Faction locks at campaign creation. Build a new warband for the next season if you want a change.

**Q: How do we handle rule interpretation disputes?**
A: Campaign organiser is the final arbiter. The design docs are authoritative.

**Q: A player joined the campaign late. How do they catch up?**
A: They start with the standard Treasury (300 gp), standard roster (build per §3 above), no Holdings, 0 Renown. They are not retroactively affected by past Annual Events. The campaign organiser may offer a "catch-up" boost (extra starting gp or Renown) for late joiners to keep the field competitive — recommend +1 Renown per previous meeting they missed, capped at +3.

**Q: Are we supposed to write a saga journal?**
A: Encouraged but not required. The Saga Journal Annual Event (AE30) rewards warbands with completed Legendary Boasts, Titles, and Holdings — these are the seeds of a written saga. Many groups find that writing 2-3 paragraphs per meeting (the deeds of your Jarl, the names of fallen Named Characters) is more fun than they expected.

**Q: My Chieftain just died. Now what?**
A: Pick a different General as Chieftain for your next battle. No formal succession needed — the Chieftain title is per-battle. If all your Generals are dead, recruit a fresh one for 50 gp from Treasury. They start with 0 XP. The new Chieftain gets a free Title roll their first battle as a recognition of fresh leadership.

**Q: Can two warbands of the same faction fight each other (Feuds Mode)?**
A: Yes — intra-faction feuds are normal in the Viking age and many other settings. Honor your in-fiction grievance.

## 8. Quick reference card

### Battle composition (each battle):
1. **Propose** — Battle Type, Location, role, target
2. **Respond** — accept clean / with Twist / counter / refuse
3. **Weather** — d6
4. **Play**

### Post-game (12 steps):
1. Wound Rolls
2. Tally Renown
3. Collect Treasure
4. Ransom + Relic Transfer
5. Saga Roll (d100)
6. Divide Shares
7. Mead-Hall (optional)
8. Recruit + Re-equip
9. Advancement (XP)
10. Title Check
11. Holdings Updates
12. Draw next Boasts

### Three currencies:
- **Treasure (gp)** — pays for replacements, equipment, Stores, ransoms
- **XP** — Named Characters earn it personally; spend on Talents/Skills/Stats/Sagas
- **Renown** — the campaign leaderboard; also spendable on Bards, Feasts, Sagas, Boasts

### Three character types:
- **General Named Character** (1-5 per warband) — class-flex; one is Chieftain each battle
- **Class-Locked Specialists** (max 1 each: Healer, Banner-bearer, Warhorn-bearer)
- **Rank-and-file warriors** — fresh per battle, not persistent

### Renown never goes negative. Holdings can be challenged. Sagas are forever.
