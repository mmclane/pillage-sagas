## 2. New scenarios to write (v0.1 raid types)

Drawn from the Dux Britanniarum D6 raid table, SimpleMinis archetypes, and original Viking-era scenarios. Each needs a v0.1 card with the standard fields: terrain layout, army budgets (often asymmetric), turn limit, victory conditions, loot/Renown rewards, special rules.

### Confirmed for v0.1

- **Cattle Raid** — Attacker must drive 2 of 3 livestock bases off the opposite table edge. Uses Pillage's prisoner-escort movement rules for livestock.
- **Wagon Train Ambush** — Attacker ambushes a slow-moving wagon column. Defender must escort the wagons across the table; attacker must capture or destroy them.
- **Coast Watch Tower** — Attacker tries to silence a watch tower before reinforcements arrive (rolling reinforcement timer).
- **Hall-Burning** — Attacker must set fire to all of a great hall's buildings (per Christ's Sandals template, but on a small scale). Asymmetric defender budget.
- **Rescue the Hostage** — Attacker must extract a specific captive figure from the defender's deployment zone and exit their own edge.
- **Defend the Longship** — Inverse of Landing: defender is the raider returning to their longship, which the attacker must reach and burn before the raiders can launch.
- **Rival Warband Feud** — Two equally-budgeted warbands meet on neutral ground for a personal score-settling. No loot; pure Renown for kills and objectives.
- **Pillage Town** — Updated/expanded version of the core Pillage! scenario, with denser loot and specific named loot tokens worth extra Renown.

### Stretch goals for v0.2

- **Sacking a Monastery** — full version with relic generation, loot tokens, and a small civilian model count.
- **Burn the Crops** — agricultural raid; defender must protect haystacks and fields from arson.
- **Slave Hunt** — capture multiple civilian figures and escort them off-table.
- **Saga Duel** — Jarl-vs-Jarl ceremonial fight with retinues that can intervene under specific triggers. Generates major Renown and a Reputation.
- **Burning Ship** — escape a longship that catches fire mid-game (using the fire spreading rules).
- **Funeral Raid** — attack a rival warband during a funeral mood, with morale modifiers.
- **The Reaving Season** — small open-table free-for-all for multi-player Feuds Mode meetings.

## 4. Asymmetric balance principles

Many raid scenarios are deliberately asymmetric (attacker vs defender, with the defender often at lower budget or unfavorable terrain). Key principles for v0.1 balance:

- **Match opportunity, not strength.** A defender at 60% of the attacker's budget should have terrain, positioning, or victory-condition advantages that make their job achievable.
- **Asymmetric victory metrics.** The attacker's win condition often differs in *kind* from the defender's. Attacker wants loot/kills/arson; defender wants survival/extraction/denial.
- **Renown rewards scale with risk.** Pulling off a raid against a fortified defender is worth more Renown than winning a balanced Pitched Battle.
- **Volunteer to be raider.** In Two Sides Mode or Holdings Mode, the raid-vs-defender choice is built into who initiated the battle. In Feuds Mode, players should agree which role they want at the scenario draw, or roll for it.
